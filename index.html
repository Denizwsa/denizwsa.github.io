FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Wsamiaw is built for Minecraft 1.8.9 with Forge. It includes a ViaVersion integration that lets you spoof your protocol version to connect to servers running different versions (requires server-side ViaVersion support).

Your license key is tied to your hardware ID. On first login, the client generates a unique HWID from your system components. Each key supports a limited number of HWID resets if you change hardware.
